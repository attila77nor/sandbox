# Build tools for the Analytika concepts

Plain Node scripts (Node 22, no dependencies; `site-shot.cjs` needs Playwright).
Run them from anywhere; paths resolve relative to this folder.

- `site-build.mjs` writes the nine clickable pages and `preview.html` to `../concepts/site/` (the dark serif look with the Galton-board stand-in). Running it again reproduces the committed files byte for byte.
- `library-wall.mjs` writes `../concepts/library/index.html`: a drawn library wall with five chalkboards. It is a stand-in for the photograph that will replace it. All formulas and curves on the boards are correct mathematics.
- `figures.mjs` holds the five maths figures (survival curve, Lorenz curve, star graph, decision tree, logistic curve), the illustrative Gompertz law and the area and page lists.
- `lib.mjs` splits `../assets/logo/logo.svg` (converted from the supplied PDF) into mark and wordmark symbols that can be recoloured with CSS variables.
- `art.mjs` draws the Galton board stand-in.
- `site-shot.cjs` takes screenshots and click-tests the site (`SHOTS=<folder> node site-shot.cjs`).
- `explore-r1-*.mjs` write round 1 of the exploration to `../concepts/explore/`: four new directions (`oslo`, `tabell`, `trekant`, `tetthet`), each a home page and a People page, plus `index.html` (`explore-r1-index.mjs`). `explore-shared.mjs` holds what they share. `explore-r1-shots.cjs` takes the screenshots into `concepts/explore/img/` (`MOBILE=1` also takes 390 px full-page shots).
- `explore-r2-vegg.mjs` writes the three home pages built on `../assets/photo/tavler.jpg` (`vegg-hvit`, `vegg-marine`, `vegg-zoom`); `explore-r2-shots.cjs` takes their screenshots. Board positions on the photograph are measured in pixels at the top of the script.
- `explore-r3-forside.mjs` writes `../concepts/explore/forside.html`, the landing page with the photograph over the whole screen that dissolves into white and four grey rows; `explore-r3-shots.cjs` takes its screenshots.
- `photo-neutral.py IN OUT [--grain STD] [--lift GAMMA] [--scale N]` (Python, needs Pillow and numpy) turns a warm-toned black-and-white photograph into neutral greys, adds a little clarity and film grain. `--grain 2.2` matches the first whiteboard photograph. Run it on the final photograph at its final size, so the grain stays fine.
- `explore-r3-forside.mjs` now writes three landing pages, one per photograph: `forside` (first whiteboard photo), `forside-rom` (the Oslo room, not dimmed, title at the bottom right) and `forside-kontor` (the open-plan office, dimmed).
- `oversikt.mjs` writes `../oversikt.html`: one self-contained file that shows every page and every sketch we have made (also each sketch inside the older files that hold several, and the earlier versions of the site pages), the photographs and the inspiration images, grouped by page type, with thumbnails. A click opens the page inside the file, scrolled to the sketch, and its links keep working. What is shown is listed in `oversikt-data.mjs` (add a page there with a section, a sub-heading and a status). `oversikt-historikk.mjs` pulls the earlier site versions out of git into `../concepts/historikk/`. `oversikt-thumbs.cjs` takes the thumbnails into `../concepts/thumbs/` and reads each sketch's title and text from its page (run it first when a page has changed; needs Playwright and python3 with Pillow; takes a few minutes). Order: the `explore-*` scripts, then the thumbnails, then `oversikt.mjs`.
- Round 4 (`explore-r3-forside.mjs`, `forside-liv-*`): the lived-in office photograph (`ekte-kontor-v5-neutral.jpg`) with four ways to place the title. The landing pages now take a `zoom`, a focus point in the photograph and a target position on the screen, so the framing is computed in the browser; `oversikt.html` has zoom buttons (1.0, 1.3, 1.5, 1.8) that tell the page to re-frame. Run `explore-r3-forside.mjs`, then `ONLY=<ids> node oversikt-thumbs.cjs` for just the new pages, then `oversikt.mjs`.
- Round 5, the crop analysis: `crop-analysis.py [PHOTO] [OUTDIR]` (Python, Pillow and numpy) scores every crop of the office photograph on nine criteria (boards size, foreground placement and size, distance between them, resolution, title contrast, free space, edges, life), shakes the weights to see which winners are stable, and measures how much blur the five boards take. It writes `../concepts/utsnitt/crop-analysis.json` and the overview images; the result is written up in `../concepts/utsnitt/README.md`. `photo-dof.py IN OUT --focus X0,Y0,X1,Y1 --sigma S` fakes a shallow depth of field (a blur ramp around the sharp box, grain put back on the blurred parts) and made the nine `assets/photo/ekte-kontor-v5-dof-*` files (s1 work desk `0,606,720,824`, s2 right desk `1283,498,1672,786`, s3 dining table `800,500,1160,700 --up 35`; sigma 2.5, 4.5, 7 = lett, middels, sterk). `explore-r3-forside.mjs` writes the four `forside-utsnitt-*` pages from them: the blurred picture fills the screen, the sharp one sits only inside the five boards and fades in when one is pointed at, and the zoom is lowered on screens where it would push the boards or the sign over them out of view. `oversikt.html` has zoom buttons (the page's own, 1.0-1.8) and blur buttons (none, light, medium, strong).
- Round 6, `explore-r6-fokus.mjs` writes `../concepts/explore/forside-fokus.html`: the landing page built with the gsap, transitions.dev and make-interfaces-feel-better skills. GSAP 3.15 and ScrollTrigger (`../assets/vendor/gsap/`) are inlined, so it is still one file. A timeline fades the evenly soft frame (`ekte-kontor-v5-dof-alle-sterk.jpg`, made with `photo-dof.py --all --sigma 10`) into the work-desk frame on arrival, the title rises in with transitions.dev's texts reveal, and one tooltip travels between the boards. A scrubbed ScrollTrigger timeline lets the focus slip away into white, and `ScrollTrigger.batch` raises the rows in, 80 ms apart. `gsap.matchMedia()` skips the arrival and the movement for reduced motion. `oversikt.html` has a "Spill av" button that replays the arrival; the thumbnail script now renders with reduced motion, so thumbnails show the finished state.
- Round 6 now builds six pages from one engine, differing only in how the word "Analytika" is set (`KINDS` in `explore-r6-fokus.mjs`): `forside-fokus` (a sign over the boards, rejected), `forside-tittel-logotype` and `-serif` (one word across the whole width at the bottom), `-logo` (the logo lockup, small, top left), `-liten-serif` (a small serif word bottom left) and `-vindu` (the logotype as a window onto the room that opens until the picture fills the screen, then rests as the small lockup; an SVG mask scaled by GSAP). `lib.mjs` now also exports the wordmark on its own (`logoWordDef`, `WORD_ASPECT`, `WORD_LETTERS`). The serif is Cormorant Garamond, embedded from `../assets/vendor/fonts/` (Impeccable's detector flags Instrument Serif as an overused face). Stills: `../concepts/utsnitt/titler-oversikt.jpg`, `titler-mobil.jpg`.
- Round 6, `forside-vindu-skroll` (kind `scroll-window` in `explore-r6-fokus.mjs`): the word is a window onto the room and stays one, nothing opens by itself. The five area names sit small at the bottom. Scrolling (one scrubbed GSAP timeline, 1.9 screens) grows the letters around the I in even steps of scale while the white around them fades, turns the names and the logo white over the picture, holds on the picture alone (the boards work again), then fades the picture into the rows. Reduced motion drops the growing and keeps the crossfade. Stills: `../concepts/utsnitt/vindu-skroll-trinn.jpg`, `vindu-skroll-mobil.jpg`.


- `oversikt.html` is now the only file to open: the whole portfolio in one place. A list on the left holds every page, grouped by page type and round with the newest round first, and stays on screen while a page is open, so switching is one click (or the arrow keys). The overview itself has a filter (all, chosen, alternative, earlier, rejected) and two card sizes. The old `concepts/explore/preview.html` and its builder `explore-bundle.mjs` were removed: it only held the explore pages and needed a tab bar that no longer fit.
- Round 7, `explore-r7-korridor.mjs` writes `../concepts/explore/forside-korridor.html`: a recreation, for Analytika, of a hero shot Atle sent as a reference (luke-peake's Bespoke Group page: a dark laptop screen in a softly lit hall, one tiny figure, nav bar, headline). It is one inline SVG in the reference's own coordinates (936 x 702, positions and light levels measured from the reference image) with the text as HTML on top. Changed on request: a cool palette (three, set with a ramp of CSS variables `--l0`-`--l95` so the lightness structure of the reference is kept: varm blå, blågrå, lilla; the buttons under the shot switch them, or `#graa` / `#lilla` in the address), a group of people, the white logo filling the black void, the headline only "Analytika", the five areas at the bottom. Fonts embedded from `../assets/vendor/fonts/` (Hanken Grotesk Light and Regular, OFL). This is the reference's composition, so for a public site it should be made our own first.
- Round 8, `explore-r8-pixel.mjs` writes `../concepts/explore/forside-pixel.html`: a recreation, for Analytika, of a reference page Atle sent (party.tokyo-style: flat saturated ground, a light card with a stair-stepped colour shape cut into it, a bold word, thin lines that turn into pixel letters, a dark pill menu). Our colours (blue `#1B92D0` as the ground and the cut-out, marine for the pills, a light tint of the blue for the bands), the logo in white on the blue above the card, "Analytika" as the bold word, only "Consulting" and "Business development" as the thin lines, and the five areas in the dark pill at the bottom right (where the reference has its menu). The stair shape is an actuarial graph on a 23 x 13 grid: a survival curve (the area above it), a cumulative claims development curve, or a run-off triangle; the buttons under the page switch them (`#overlevelse`, `#trekant` in the address; the claims development curve is the default). The pixel letters come from a 5 x 7 bitmap font in the script, the regular ones are Hanken Grotesk Medium. The reference's Japanese caption is left out. As with round 7, this is the reference's layout, so make it our own before it goes public.
- Round 9, `explore-r9-studio.mjs` writes `../concepts/explore/forside-studio.html`: a recreation, for Analytika, of a hero shot Atle sent (a dark, soft-edged frame with a photograph, a small pill at the top, a big white headline at the bottom left, a glass card at the bottom right).
