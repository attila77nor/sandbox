About

Analytika AS is an Oslo firm of actuaries, pricing specialists, data engineers and consultants. We work for the insurers of Norway, and some abroad.

The work falls in five areas: Actuary, Pricing, Data platforms, Consulting and Business development. Each has a chapter below.

The firm was started so that smart people would have a place to get better at what they love.

011. Actuary

Reserving, risk and compliance.

Four fields: risk actuary, compliance, reserving, and life and pension. Building and reviewing the models that decide how much capital an insurer has to hold, making the Solvency II framework work from day to day, and an independent view on whether the reserves are sufficient.

022. Pricing

Advanced tariffs and pricing models.

Tariff development and analytics for insurers: the rating factors, the models behind them, and what a change in them does to the portfolio.
